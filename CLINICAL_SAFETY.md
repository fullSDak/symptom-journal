# Clinical and privacy boundaries

Symptom Trail is a fictional-data prototype for patient-authored observations, not a diagnostic system or urgent-care service. No data entered here is automatically reviewed by a clinician. A negative ANA does not rule out every inflammatory condition, but the tool cannot interpret an individual's ANA result. See the [American College of Rheumatology](https://rheumatology.org/patients/antinuclear-antibodies-ana).

Visible skin symptoms can look different on different skin tones. Users may record their own observations and choose photos; the prototype does not assess images, infer race, diagnose a rash or replace an examination. The [American Academy of Dermatology](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/eczema/types/atopic-dermatitis/symptoms) describes such differences for eczema, not a rule for all rashes. Do not photograph intimate areas in this prototype; ask a clinician how to document them safely.

If a patient chooses to include a reported hemoglobin disorder or red-cell finding, the summary may optionally say: “Red-cell size and shape, and anemia, may affect the erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR). Please interpret results in the context of my blood count and clinical history.” This does not mean every hemoglobin disorder or target cell makes ESR unusable, or predict the direction of its effect. ESR is influenced by several red-cell and inflammatory factors. [American Family Physician](https://www.aafp.org/pubs/afp/issues/1999/1001/p1443.html); [University of Washington laboratory guidance](https://menu.labmed.washington.edu/view/ESR).

For severe difficulty breathing or swallowing, or sudden swelling of the lips, mouth, tongue or throat, contact local emergency services immediately rather than waiting to log symptoms. [NHS anaphylaxis guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/anaphylaxis/).

Browser-only storage is not encryption, a medical record system, HIPAA compliance or a guarantee of safety. Records may be erased with browser data. Browser speech recognition may use an external service and must be separately opted into. Share only through channels you choose and trust. Many consumer health applications do not fall under HIPAA; rules vary by product and jurisdiction. See [HHS guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/cell-phone-hipaa/index.html) and [FTC guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-ftcs-health-breach-notification-rule-0).
